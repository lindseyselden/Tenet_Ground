## What changed

The commercial model is stated for the first time, and one canonical file is re-read in its light.

**The model.** Tenet Ground licenses a platform whose operation is restricted to certified Integrity
Architects. A buyer acquires the technology and the implementation together. Architects may be
employed by Tenet Ground or independently qualified — the certification travels with the person, not
the employer. Tenet Ground holds the standard and licenses the platform; it does not sell seats and it
is not a consultancy. Specified in `audience.md`.

**The Ground Hall is a certified environment, not Tenet Ground's own building.** A Ground Hall is any
threshold that meets the standard. The identical receptacles are a specification, not an interior
decision. `ground-hall.md`'s description is unchanged — only its status changes, from *our lobby* to
*the spec any qualifying threshold is built to*.

**Three things are certified, not one:** the practitioner, the instrument (the Ground Ring), and the
environment (the Ground Hall).

## Why

The model was never stated, and two readings had been running in parallel: Tenet Ground as a software
vendor whose customers operate the product, and Tenet Ground as a firm whose staff operate it for
them. Neither survives the brand's own thesis.

The reason sits in `messaging.md` pillar 3, which was written as positioning and turns out to be the
commercial logic: *the Integrity Architect's own signature is the one thing a deepfake can't
reproduce.* **In an economy of Digital Twin Deception, software alone cannot be the ground truth,
because software can be replicated.** Only an instrumented, continuously verified human can anchor it.
The certified practitioner is therefore structurally load-bearing — remove them and the platform's
output has no authority. This is not a services layer sold alongside a product; it is the product's
condition of validity.

Every asset already assumed this without saying it. The Ground Ring reads its wearer's own behavioral
baseline. The Ground Sanctum verifies the person before releasing the instrument. Both are unintelligible
under a pure-SaaS model and obvious under this one.

The certification was scoped **broad** — a profession Tenet Ground defines and others may qualify
into — rather than narrow, where Tenet Ground employs every Architect. Three reasons:

1. `ground-hall.md` already reads as a specification rather than a description ("every receptacle is
   identical... the architecture imposes uniformity on the system"). The world was built broad before
   the model was named.
2. The manifesto's *"If it isn't verified by Tenet Ground, it isn't viable"* is the sentence of a
   standards body, not of a firm.
3. It makes `Ground Standard` — flagged as a possible future in `lexicon.md` — an actual asset.

The accepted consequence: **Tenet Ground is not the hero of its own brand.** The Architect is. The
institution stands behind them, recessive, which is Rule #01 applied to brand architecture rather than
to a photograph.

## What this supersedes

Nothing — the model was previously unstated rather than stated differently.

`foundation.md`'s use of *platform* stands and is correct: this is a platform, gated at the operator
layer. An earlier reading that "platform" should become "institution" was considered and rejected —
the technology is real and licensed; what is restricted is who may operate it.

Asset-level specs are unaffected. `ground-hall.md` and `ground-ring.md` need no edits; their content
was already written as specification.
